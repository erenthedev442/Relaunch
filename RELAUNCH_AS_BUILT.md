## 1. Quick orientation

**Core loop.** A new character is handed a near-complete account (all spells/WS/trusts/maps/missions) and 300k gil, then climbs a token-driven endgame: **Hunting League ranks → Reforge armor → Prestige/Ascension → infinite Apex/Paragon**, funded by farming custom NMs for marks and spending them at token vendors. Retail leveling/gil is largely bypassed; progression is the custom systems.

**The currencies** (everything routes through these):

| Currency | Stored as | Earned from | Spent on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gil** | engine | 300k starter, content, currency cash-out, AH bot | Big sinks: Prime forge 750M, Race Changer 100M, Casino, gacha, warps, titles |
| **Hunt Marks** | charVar `HL_Points` | HL NM kills, daily/streak logins, Daily Board, crafting | Medal trio, Sortie earrings, the 3 Void-Keeper custom trusts, NM-affinity registration; also the Ascension ascend cost |
| **Medal trio** (Beastmens/Kindreds/Demons) | items 9539/9541/9543 | bought with Hunt Marks (5/15/40) | Armor / Weapons / Accessory gear vendors (Bronze/Silver/Gold) |
| **Infamy** | charVar `Infamy` | Invasion wins, weekly Raid, Gauntlet, Maat, Abyssea | Infamy Vendor (BiS gear) — the deliberately-scarce top currency |
| **Reforge marks** (AF/Relic/Empy) | charVars `RF_*_Marks` | Reforge NM kills, Daily Board | Reforge upgrades (+1/+2/+3), Mark Exchange |
| **Paragon Points** | charVar `Paragon_Points` | Apex Trials clears, Gauntlet | Paragon board (infinite levels + capped perks) |
| **Allied Notes** | engine | Endless Tower trash, [S]-zone kills | Cosmetic Shop |
| **Mastery Sigils** | charVar `MasterySigils` | daily NM rotation (50 per live target) + small trickle on any NM | Spell & Skill Mastery — WS/spell potency, WS-effect procs, trait riders |

**Where things live.** The Hunting League hub and gear vendors are in **Escha–Zi'Tah**. The old GM-Home service NPCs were relocated for relaunch (commit `a237cb84e4`): "beginner" services → **Celennia Memorial Library**, "endgame" services → **Leafallia**. The three **Escha-RuAun (zone 289)** content NPCs were spread far apart for relaunch so concurrent groups don't collide, and **each is now placed as 3 interchangeable copies** (~8 units apart) so several players can start independent runs at once: the **Voidspire Warden** sits on the **south / entry plaza**, the **Star-Devourer "Voidgate Sentinel" (Raid)** in the **north** area, and the **Game Master** (`!wavemaster` wave-survival system) in the **west** area. (Exact zone IDs/coords are in flux in the catalogs pending an in-game pass — treat NPC locations here as approximate.)

## 2. The player journey — progression

### 2.1 A new character (creation → first login)

On first login a char is brought to near-complete state automatically:

- **Upstream baseline** (`scripts/globals/player.lua`): race/job starting gear, nation map, job-gesture KIs, starting title; level 1, level cap = `INITIAL_LEVEL_CAP` (default 50).
- **`new_player_starter.lua`** — one-time **300,000 gil** gift (`STARTER_GIL=300000`), gated by charVar `Starter_Gift`. (A relaunch addition; *not* clawed back by the economy nerfs.)
- **`new_char_starter_marks.lua`** — **25 Hunt Marks** (`STARTER_MARKS=25`) — enough for one entry-level Bronze weapon — plus a welcome MOTD.
- **`Character_Upgrader.lua`** — 3 s after first login, `giveEverything()` grants: **all weapon skills, all spells (1-1024, incl. every BLU spell), all skills capped, all trusts, all quests + missions complete, all maps/outpost warps/122 homepoints/survival guides, all 8 wardrobes sized up, all automaton parts.** Excludes only the 5 paid Void-Keeper trust IDs (899/901/902/930/1007). *(The old on-demand "Unlocker" NPC was removed `eea34e083e`; this now runs automatically.)*
- **`unlock_adoulin_jobs.lua`** — RUN + GEO unlocked for everyone (selectable in the Mog House menu). ⚠ GEO still has the retail "0 MP" bug unless the C++/SQL GEO restore is deployed on the box (unconfirmable from Lua).
- **`unlock_progression_keyitems.lua`** — grants **LIMIT_BREAKER (606)** + **JOB_BREAKER (2544)** so the Merit and Job Point menus aren't greyed (players hit 99 via custom systems, not retail genkai). ⚠ Capacity Points still don't accrue (Lv100+ NMs set `NO_CAPACITY_POINTS`); merits work once un-greyed.

> ⚠ **Wardrobe conflict to clean up:** three modules fight over wardrobe sizing at `charCreate` (`new_char_wardrobe_sizes` sets all 8 to full, `mission_wardrobe_unlocks` zeroes them, `Character_Upgrader` bumps all 8 on a 3 s timer). The Character_Upgrader timer almost certainly wins, but it's order-fragile.

### 2.2 Leveling to 99

Retail leveling is intact but supplemented. *(Relaunch removed the always-on "Gain Experience" RoE module and the `!gainexp` instant-claim command; Records of Eminence revert to the retail rotation, with no forced/on-demand EXP bundle.)*

- **`!expcamp`** — replaces the retired ExpCamp Moogle; lists 20 level-banded camps, `!expcamp <N>` warps you there.
- **`subjob_exp_share.lua`** — banks **25%** of *every* EXP gain toward your active subjob, auto-leveling it in the background (capped at main-job level). Curve mirrors the real `exp_base` table. Switching subs wipes the bank (no transfer). Jobs that have been through Job Rebirth are excluded (must be re-grinded as a main). *(Relaunch cut the share rate 50%→25%; the in-file "0.5" comments are now stale.)*
- **`blu_spell_progression.lua`** — auto-grants BLU spells by level (and a full catch-up on login); backed by `blu_spells.sql` which adds 23 missing SOA BLU spells.

### 2.3 The endgame ladder (the core spine)

**Step 1 — Hunting League** (`HuntingLeague.lua`, hub = Escha–Zi'Tah, enter with **`!hunt`**). A 5-rank NM ladder; each rank has 3 NMs you spawn from a tier NPC. Unlocking rank N (paid in Hunt Marks at the Seals NPC) also gates whether you *earn* marks from that rank.

| Rank | Unlock cost (Hunt Marks) | NMs | Base marks/kill |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Initiate | 0 | Leaping Lizzy, Valkurm Emperor, Tom Tit Tat | 5 |
| II — Hunter | 150 | Roc, Bomb Queen, Aquarius | 12 |
| III — Elite | 650 | Serket, Vrtra, Simurgh | 22 |
| IV — Champion | 1,500 | Nidhogg, King Behemoth, Kirin | 38 |
| V — Legend | 3,000 | Absolute Virtue, Pandemonium Warden, **Shinryu** | 65 (Shinryu 110) |

Cumulative to Legend = **5,300 marks** (relaunch re-priced the rank gates up from the old 75/225/500/1000). NMs are Lv150 (Shinryu Lv225-250) with big HP boosts (6×→36×, Shinryu 72×). Mark payout stacks bonuses: **first-kill +100%, weekly Featured Hunt +200%, party +25-50%, kill-streak +10-50%**, all multiplied by your Hunter's Guild amplifier and any seasonal bonus. *(Relaunch Phase 2 cut NM ATT/DEF/REGEN hard vs Legendary; HP unchanged.)*

The **HL reward shop** is the bridge to gear: it sells the medal trio (Beastmens 5 / Kindreds 15 / Demons 40 marks) plus Sortie earrings (NQ 100 / +1 200). ⚠ **Stale shop rows:** the catalog still lists a "Spells" category (Silencega 200, Convergence 350, Divine Aegis 400) left over from the now-removed custom-spell system (§4.2) — but those rows are dead: `learnSpell` 1020 (Divine Aegis) and 1021 (Convergence) no longer exist in `spell_list` (only stock Silencega 359 survives), so buying them teaches nothing usable. Clean up `hunting_league_catalog.lua` lines ~583-590.

**Step 2 — Reforge armor** (`Reforge_System.lua`, zone = Gwora-Corridor). Three parallel NM pools → three currencies → three AF/Relic/Empy armor sets, base → +3:

| Set / currency | NM ladder (Lv150→250) | marks/kill |
|---|---|---|
| **AF** (`RF_AF_Marks`) | Genbu→Suzaku→Seiryu→Byakko→Kirin | 25/40/60/90/150 |
| **Relic** (`RF_Relic_Marks`) | Bukhis→Khun→Padfoot→Glavoid→Tinnin | 25/40/60/90/150 |
| **Empy** (`RF_Empy_Marks`) | Aello→Iratham→Briareus→Itzpapalotl→Hadhayosh | 25/40/60/90/150 |

Each kill drops a random base piece (50% biased to your main job, cutting a set grind to ~50-80 kills) + the set's marks. Upgrade costs (own currency, identical across AF/Relic/Empy): **base→+1 300, +1→+2 900, +2→+3 2,000** (3,200 total/piece). A Mark Exchange NPC converts between mark types with a tax (2 AF→1 Relic, 2 Relic→1 Empy, 3 AF→1 Empy).

**Step 3 — Prestige / Ascension** (`Prestige_System.lua`, Altar in Provenance). **Per-job** infinite prestige, gated behind **HL Tier 5 (Legend)**. The cycle: clear the current "Nightmare Court" Trial (3 bosses), then pay an escalating Hunt Mark cost (`min(500×(level+1), 3000)`) to ascend = +1 prestige level on the current job + an AP grant (10 AP at P.Lv 1-50, 15 at 51-80, 20 at 81+). Prestige level is **uncapped**; AP buys ~40 permanent stat categories (each **capped** — e.g. Haste +1%/lv cap 15%, ATT +20/lv cap 50, WS Dmg +2%/lv cap 20%), so power plateaus even as level climbs.

The **Trial roster scales with prestige level** — one Court per 10 levels:

| P.Lv | Court | Bosses |
|---|---|---|
| 0-9 | The Nightmare Court | Diabolos, Medusa, Odin |
| 10-19 | The Voidwalkers | Sarameya, Kaggen, Qilin |
| 20-29 | The Jailers | Jailers of Justice / Fortitude / Temperance |
| 30-39 | The Voidwalker Lords | Kreutzet, Raja, Maere |
| 40-59 | The World's End | Omega, Ultima, Provenance Watcher |
| 60+ | The Celestial Wardens | Tiamat, Kirin, Absolute Virtue |

Ascension **no longer carries any EXP penalty** — relaunch removed it (the engine's `[PrestigeExpCut]` charVar is now always set to 0 on every job-mod refresh; the old −4%/level-to-−80%-cap `expCut()` is dead code left in place for history). *(Relaunch Phase 2 also cut the tier-0 Court's ATT/DEF/REGEN hard; higher tiers unchanged. The cosmetic Ascension_Companion pet is **DISABLED**.)*

### 2.4 Infinite & repeatable endgame

- **Apex Trials** (Apex Arbiter NPC; arena = Walk of Echoes [P2] z279; `!apex` is status/abort only). Greater-Rift-style infinite solo climb; one boss per tier. The three-band curve targets Relic + T5 through T50, prepared Prime/final REMA through T100, then logarithmic elite scaling: level 135→145→150, HP 1.5M→7.54M→20.29M→about 67.27M at T500, with int16-safe stat anchors and affixes at T25/50/75/100/200/300. Mechanics reach the Prime kit at T100 and the full Absolute kit at T200. Reward: **Paragon Points = 10 + 5×(tier-1)** per first clear; raises your `Apex_HighestTier`. Bosses spawn 22-30y from the owner, remain non-aggroable to other climbers, and engage their owner after 4 seconds.
- **Paragon board** (`Paragon.lua` / Paragon Sage). Spends Paragon Points: **Ascend** (infinite `Paragon_Level`, cost `25+5×cur`) + four capped perks (Vigor +HP cap 5000, Might +ATT/RATT cap 1000, Precision +ACC/RACC cap 1000, Warding +DEF cap 2000) + a once-per-day **Daily Might** 2 h buff (unlock 80 PP). Perks re-apply every login.
- **Endless Tower** (`!tower` / Tower Arbiter, Walk of Echoes z182). 50-floor solo climb, single-occupant server-wide. Bosses every 10th floor; floor-50 clear sets **Prime Weapon Trial 2**. Rewards Allied Notes per trash kill. ⚠ **Bug: the floor-50 boss is set to level 275, which wraps past uint8 255 → its base stats are accidentally low** (7M HP still makes it a wall, but its accuracy/TP-move damage are far under intent).
- **Job Mastery** (`!mastery` / Weapon Mastery Sage, Walk of Echoes z182). 12 weapon-Guardian solo fights (Lv160/170, 50-55M HP, brutal affixes). Any one kill sets **Prime Weapon Trial 4**.
- **The Gauntlet** (The Gauntlet NPC → Riverne-Site A01). 10-level solo NM run; levels 1-9 offer a Safe Path or a fight, level 10 is Shinryu-or-expulsion. HP doubles per level (L1 20k → L10 ~10.2M); **does not use the shared mechanics engine** (pure stat block). Clearing level 10 pays **5,000,000 gil + 500 Paragon Points + 500 Infamy** and enshrines a permanent named NPC in the Hall of Champions (Riverne-Site B01). *(Relaunch cut this reward 99% from the old 500M gil / 50k PP / 50k Infamy — it is no longer the server's biggest one-shot payout.)*
- **Prime Armory** (Prime Armory NPC). Forge one Prime weapon per character behind 5 trials (Abyssea collectibles ×12, **Tower floor 50**, a Prime Voucher, **a Weapon Guardian**, and 99× three currency items) + **750,000,000 gil** — the largest gil sink on the server. 12 custom Primes (each with a custom WS) + 16 relic final forms.

### 2.5 Job Rebirth (parallel prestige)

`JobRebirth.lua` (`!rebirth`, NPC under Ru'Lude Gardens). Independent of Ascension (separate currency/caps; the two **stack**). Requires the current job's **spent Job Points ≥ 2,100** (maxed). Rebirthing wipes the job's JP, sets it to **level 1**, and grants accelerating **Rebirth Points** (RP, ~10 at R1 climbing with +30 milestone bonuses every 10). RP buys the same per-job stat categories as Ascension (separate storage/caps). Each rebirth stamps a multiplicative **EXP penalty** (−4% at R1 to a −90% cap), so re-grinding gets progressively harder. Uncapped rebirths. Surfaced (with Ascension, JP, merits, gear) in the **`!reallevel`** "Real Level" leaderboard metric and the **master-star color** (silver→red, 8 tiers).

## 4. Character power — augments & job overhauls

### 4.1 The augment system

Trade gear + 1-5 NM-drop "catalyst" items to the **Augment Moogle** (flat **10,000 gil/trade**). One catalyst = one augment slot; the engine sums slots as `(value + boost) × multiplier`, with **boost a 0-31 roll**. Boost quality = **Sage mastery × NM affinity × crit**:

- **Mastery (5 ranks)** gated on relaunch by **content milestones** (HL Tier 2/3/5, then Prestige Lv 15/30), multiplier 1.0→2.0×, crit 5→20%. *(The old seal/trophy gates are dead code.)*
- **NM Affinities** — permanently give a stat category a **×1.5** multiplier. **Relaunch-gated (2026-06-25):** no longer a free trophy turn-in — registration now requires **Hunting League Rank 3 (Elite)** *and* **1,000 Hunt Marks** (spent), *on top of* consuming the NM trophy. One of 13 categories.
- **Crit token** — holding Maat's Cap (item 15194) guarantees the 2× crit (consumed). Max realistic multiplier = 2.0 (rank) × 1.5 (affinity) × 2.0 (crit) = **6.0**.

Catalog design rule: job/pet/resist/skill augments are free (tier 0); universally-strong stats (Haste, DA/TA/QA, PDT/MDT, Dmg+, HP/MP, crit-dmg, TP/WS) are tier 3-4 (require higher Sage rank).

**Where catalysts drop — 1:1 specific mobs (2026-06-25).** Each catalyst drops from exactly **one mob**, and **no mob drops more than one catalyst** (`augment_catalyst_mobs.lua` — a tier-banded 1:1 auto-assignment — applied by the `augment_catalyst_drops.lua` global `onMobDeathEx` handler at **50%** on the assigned mob's death). The assignment is banded to **mob level** so farm difficulty matches the augment's power, and **297 of 299 sit on a mob that already dropped the item**. This **replaced the old random tier-pool roll** (`augment_catalyst_pools.roll`, now neutered) — so HL / Prestige / Abyssea NMs no longer drop random catalysts. The full catalyst → mob mapping is in the **appendix**.

### 4.2 Combat / job overhauls (all LIVE; relaunch-nerfed where noted)

- **BST jug pets** (`BstJugPetOverhaul`, player jugs only): flat ATT +5,000 / ACC +9,000 / HP +100,000 (relaunch, down from 11,200/280,000), master-stat sharing, PDT/MDT −25%, DA/TA 20/5%, magical-Ready ×21 BP damage, and **auto-Ready every 2.5 s once TP≥1000**. "Beast Affinity" augment scales the flats. Call Beast/Bestial Loyalty recast = 0.
- **SMN avatars** (`smn_avatar_boost`, main SMN): BP damage multiplier ~95× targeting 500-750k/Blood Pact (bypasses the 131k on-screen cap), plus physical/magical/survivability flats (ATT 3,000, HP +50,000 — relaunch, down from 6,000/150,000). `bp_delay_uncap` removes the 45 s BP-recast floor (down to 15 s).
- **RNG** (`RngOverhaul`, main RNG): a massive flat ranged bundle (RATT +80,000, RANGED_DMG_RATING +5,000, **DAMAGE_LIMITP +1,400 = 10× the pDIF cap**, Snapshot/Rapid/Double-Shot boosts). Barrage + Double Shot are near-permanent stances — 3 s recast (`rng_stance_recast.sql`), 12 h duration (in-place `ranger.lua` bump), no per-shot consume (C++); `ranged_no_distance_penalty` zeroes the sweet-spot penalty.
- **Cross-job** — **Ability Trainer** (10M gil/ability, up to 15; curated self/party buffs like Berserk/Meditate/Sentinel, usable via macro on any job) and **Trait Trainer** (10M gil/trait; 6 passive traits incl. **Dual Wield**, which with `!offhand` + `DualWieldPersist` lets any job equip an off-hand).
- **Enhancing magic** double duration (×2 on the final value). *(The custom spell system — `!aegis`/`!convergence`/`!silencega` commands, their spell scripts, the `custom_spell_ids`/`custom_spell_command` modules, `custom_spells.sql`, and the docgen — was **removed entirely** on relaunch. Spell IDs 1020/1021 are gone from `spell_list`; only stock Silencega 359 remains.)*

### 4.3 Trusts

- **Void Keeper** custom trusts (one NPC, `trust_skoll.lua`): **Meat** (budget tank), **Gemma** (full support/heal), **Corvus** (apex ranged DD) — they repurpose stock trust spell IDs (899/901/902), so the client menu shows the old name (Excenmille / Nanaa Mihgo / Curilla). **Relaunch: these are EARNED through Hunting League, not bought with gil** — each is gated on an HL rank *and* a Hunt Marks cost (one unlock/char, spell permanently added on payment): **Meat = Rank 2 / 2,000 marks, Gemma = Rank 3 / 3,000 marks, Corvus = Rank 4 / 5,000 marks**. ### 4.4 Spell & Skill Mastery (relaunch-new)

A WS/spell empowerment system at the **Mastery Sage** NPC in **Leafallia** (x −8, z 20), spending a new currency — **Mastery Sigils** (charVar `MasterySigils`). Buy upgrades at the NPC; **`!empower`** shows your balance + everything you own (`!empower give <n>` is a GM test faucet).

- **Earning sigils** (`spell_skill_mastery_catalog.lua`): the primary source is a **daily NM rotation** — 3 of a 20-NM overworld pool (Jaggedy-Eared Jack, King Arthro, Kraken, Nue, Guivre…) are "live" each 24 h, derived from the clock (no DB); killing a live target = **50 sigils**, once per target per period, **party-wide** (every same-zone party member). The pool is deliberately distinct from the HL / Reforge / Hunters'-Guild NM sets. A small **trickle** (2 + 0.03×mob level, cap 10) drops on any NM so you're never fully dry between rotation targets.
