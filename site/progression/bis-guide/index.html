    Best-in-Slot Guide¶

    This guide explains the priority order for gearing up on Legendary. The endgame gear hierarchy is:

    Reforge +3 > Reforge +2 > Reforge +1 > Reforge base > Silver/Gold weapon. Augments are additive on top.

    If you're new to the server, read through this once before spending marks. Mistakes are recoverable, but spending 650 Relic Marks before you know your job's track is a painful lesson.

    Priority Order (All Jobs)¶

    Follow this order regardless of job:

    1. Get a Gold-tier weapon from the Weapons Vendor at Reisenjima Henge. Gold weapons cost 50 Demons Medals each. Demons Medals are purchased with Hunt Marks (40 marks each) — so expect roughly 2,000 Hunt Marks total for a Gold weapon. This is your damage foundation and it takes about 5–6 hours of T1/T2 farming to achieve.

    2. Buy your Sortie earring (NQ) from the Hunt Accessories NPC at Reisenjima Henge. 100 Hunt Marks gets you the NQ version. This earring is job-specific and gives meaningful stats immediately.

    3. Start Reforge farming. Talk to the Spawner NPC at Gwora Corridor and pick an NM ladder that suits your job (see below). Each kill drops a random armor piece from that set's pool and grants set-specific upgrade currency.

    4. Upgrade Reforge pieces to +1, then +2, then +3. The Reforge Vendor NPC at Gwora Corridor handles upgrades. Costs are in the set's own currency: 100 marks for +1, 300 for +2, 650 for +3. Each tier is a meaningful step up.

    5. Augment your Reforge +3 pieces. Trade the appropriate catalyst to the Augment Moogle. See the Augment Priorities section below.

    6. Upgrade your Sortie earring to +1 or +2 once you have marks to spare. The +1 adds Acc/MACC +8~12 for 200 marks; the +2 improves that to +10~15 for 350 marks.

    Choosing Your Reforge Track¶

    Three NM ladders. Three separate currencies. Three distinct armor sets. You can and should pursue all three eventually — they don't compete with each other — but pick one to focus first.

    AF Track — Sky Gods (AF Marks)¶

    Ladder: Genbu (Lv150) → Suzaku (Lv175) → Seiryu (Lv200) → Byakko (Lv225) → Kirin (Lv250)

    Mark rewards per kill: 25 / 40 / 60 / 90 / 150 AF Marks

    Sets include: Pummeler's (WAR), Anchorite's (MNK), Theophany (WHM), Spaekona's (BLM), Atrophy (RDM), Pillager's (THF), Reverence (PLD), Ignominy (DRK), Totemic (BST), Brioso (BRD), Orion (RNG), Wakido (SAM), Hachiya (NIN), Vishap (DRG), Convoker's (SMN), Assimilator's (BLU), Laksamana's (COR), Foire (PUP), Maxixi (DNC), Academic's (SCH), Geomancy (GEO), Runeist (RUN)

    Best for: Jobs that benefit from the classic Sky endgame stat profile — strong melee DD sets and mage sets with balanced offense.

    Upgrade costs: 100 / 300 / 650 AF Marks per tier

    Relic Track — Unity NMs (Relic Marks)¶

    Ladder: Bukhis (Lv150) → Khun (Lv175) → Padfoot (Lv200) → Glavoid (Lv225) → Tinnin (Lv250)

    Mark rewards per kill: 25 / 40 / 60 / 90 / 150 Relic Marks

    Sets include: Agoge (WAR), Hesychast's (MNK), Piety (WHM), Archmage's (BLM), Vitiation (RDM), Plunderer's (THF), Caballarius (PLD), Fallen's (DRK), Ankusa (BST), Bihu (BRD), Arcadian (RNG), Sakonji (SAM), Mochizuki (NIN), Pteroslaver (DRG), Glyphic (SMN), Luhlaza (BLU), Lanun (COR), Pitre (PUP), Horos (DNC), Pedagogy (SCH), Bagua (GEO), Futhark (RUN)

    Best for: Jobs that favor the Relic gear profile — many of these sets have strong TP-building, accuracy, and debuff-landing bonuses.

    Upgrade costs: 100 / 300 / 650 Relic Marks per tier

    Empy Track — Abyssea NMs (Empy Marks)¶

    Ladder: Aello (Lv150) → Iratham (Lv175) → Briareus (Lv200) → Itzpapalotl (Lv225) → Hadhayosh (Lv250)

    Mark rewards per kill: 25 / 40 / 60 / 90 / 150 Empy Marks

    Sets include: Boii (WAR), Bhikku (MNK), Ebers (WHM), Wicce (BLM), Lethargic (RDM), Skulker's (THF), Chevalier's (PLD), Heathen's (DRK), Nukumi (BST), Fili (BRD), Amini (RNG), Kasuga (SAM), Hattori (NIN), Peltast's (DRG), Beckoner's (SMN), Hashishin (BLU), Chasseur's (COR), Karagoz (PUP), Maculele (DNC), Arbatel (SCH), Azimuth (GEO), Erilaz (RUN)

    Best for: Jobs that benefit from Empyrean set bonuses — many of these sets have strong WS damage modifiers and survivability stats.

    Note on loot bias

    The Reforge system has a 50% chance of dropping a piece for your current main job instead of a random piece from the 22-job pool. You don't have to game your job selection, but farming on the job you're gearing is measurably faster.

    Augment Priorities¶

    Augments from the Augment Moogle stack on top of your Reforge gear. The catalog enforces a strongest-version-only policy — every stat is available at its maximum value, so you never have to hunt a weaker version first.

    Melee DPS¶

    Priority Augment Notes
    1 DMG +97 (melee weapon) Single largest DPS increase available
    2 Attack +33 Flat attack on body or hands
    3 Accuracy +33 Floor your miss rate first
    4 WS DMG +5% (your main weaponskill) Dozens of WS-specific augments available
    5 Dbl.Atk.+1% or Triple Atk.+1% Stack on after core stats

    Ranged (RNG/COR)¶

    Priority Augment Notes
    1 DMG +97 (ranged weapon) Separate from melee DMG augment
    2 Rng.Attack +33
    3 Rng.Accuracy +33
    4 WS DMG +5% (your ranged WS)

    Tank (PLD/RUN)¶

    Priority Augment Notes
    1 Physical Damage Taken -2% Strongest defensive augment available
    2 HP +97 Pure HP on body/legs
    3 DEF +10 Stacks well with existing DEF
    4 Evasion +3 or Mag.Evasion +3

    Mage (BLM/RDM/SCH/GEO/SMN)¶

    Priority Augment Notes
    1 Mag. Acc. +33 Lands your spells on tough targets
    2 Magic Damage +1 Flat magic damage per stack
    3 Fast Cast +1% Cast time reduction
    4 MP +97 Pure MP pool on body/legs

    Healer (WHM)¶

    Priority Augment Notes
    1 Cure potency +1% Direct heal output
    2 MP +97 Pool sustainability
    3 Healing magic skill +1 Scales cure potency and land rates
    4 Enmity -1 Stay out of hate range

    Earring Tiers¶

    The Hunt Accessories NPC at Reisenjima Henge sells Sortie earrings in three power tiers. All earrings are job-specific — you're buying the version for your job.

    Tier Cost Acc/MACC Bonus
    NQ 100 Hunt Marks Base stats only (job-specific bonus)
    +1 200 Hunt Marks +8~12 Acc/MACC added
    +2 350 Hunt Marks +10~15 Acc/MACC added

    Every job has an earring. The base stat on the NQ version is job-specific (see examples below):

    • WAR: Boii Earring — Double Attack +7%, Subtle Blow +5
    • WHM: Ebers Earring — Healing Magic +10, Enmity -7
    • BLM/SCH: Wicce/Arbatel Earring — Magic ATK +7, Magic Dmg +7
    • SAM: Kasuga Earring — Store TP +7, SC Bonus +5
    • PLD: Chevalier's Earring — DEF +20, Shield Skill +10, Cure +10%
    • RDM: Lethargy Earring — Fast Cast +7%, Enhancing Magic Duration +7%

    Buy the NQ version first, then upgrade when you have marks to spare. The +2 version is the clear endgame target but the NQ is a significant upgrade from nothing.

    The Full Endgame Checklist¶

    • [ ] Hunting League Rank V (Legend)
    • [ ] Reforge +3 in all 5 slots (head/body/hands/legs/feet) for your primary job
    • [ ] Highest-tier Sortie earring (+2) for your job
    • [ ] DMG +97 augment on your main weapon (or WS DMG +5% for your primary weaponskill)
    • [ ] Attack +33 or Mag.Acc. +33 on body and hands (per your role)
    • [ ] Physical Damage Taken -2% or HP +97 on defensive pieces
    • [ ] All Weekly Hunt Board objectives completed at least once
    • [ ] Gold-tier weapon in all relevant slots

    When all boxes are checked, you're at the ceiling of what gear can do for you. From there it's about mastery, party composition, and tackling the hardest content the server has to offer.
