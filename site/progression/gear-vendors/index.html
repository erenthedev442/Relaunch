    Gear Vendors¶

    Three custom NPCs at Escha ZiTah sell endgame gear for currency you'll already be earning from regular content. All three stand on the same row next to the Hunting League NPCs in the same zone — one trip covers your full progression loop.

    NPC Location (Escha ZiTah) Sells
    Armor Vendor (-3, -0.5, -30) Head / Body / Hands / Legs / Feet
    Weapons Vendor (-6, -0.5, -30) Every weapon category, all jobs
    Accessories Vendor (-9, -0.5, -30) Neck / Earrings / Rings / Back / Waist

    Currencies¶

    Both vendors use the same three currencies, tier-gated:

    Tier Currency How to get
    Bronze Beastmens Medal 5 Hunt Marks at the Hunting League Seals NPC
    Silver Kindreds Medal 15 Hunt Marks at the Hunting League Seals NPC
    Gold Demons Medal 40 Hunt Marks at the Hunting League Seals NPC

    Buy seals in bulk

    The Seals NPC supports multi-quantity purchases — when previewing a seal, you'll see Buy 1, Buy 10, and Buy max options. The max is computed live from your mark balance and is capped at 99 per click (one full inventory stack). Click again to grab another stack.
