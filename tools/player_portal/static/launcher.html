Legendary Launcher

Play Relaunch through Ashita or Windower. The launcher never overwrites retail FFXI. It installs XIPivot, Legendary names, and keeps your overlay current.

Download LegendaryLauncher.zip
  1. Extract the zip somewhere you will keep.
  2. Run LegendaryLauncher.exe.
  3. Pick Ashita or Windower, then press Set up Legendary.
  4. Press Update if it asks, then Play.

You still need a normal FFXI install. Guide: ffxi-legendary.com/getting-started/launcher
