Job Rebirth

Prestige System

Master a job. Burn it down. Rise stronger.

When a job has nothing left to earn — level 99, every Job Point spent — Rebirth sends it back to level 1 and wipes its Job Points clean. In exchange you earn Rebirth Points: a currency you pour into permanent stat boosts. Each rebirth makes the climb harder, and your character permanently stronger.

What a Rebirth Does

🔥 The Sacrifice

  • Level resets to 1. You re-level the job from scratch.
  • Job Points fully wiped — every category, gift, and capacity point on that job is cleared to zero.
  • Only the reborn job is affected. Your other jobs are untouched.

✦ The Reward

  • +10 Rebirth Points banked for that job.
  • Spent on permanent stat boosts that survive every future rebirth.
  • Boosts are per-job — active only while you're playing the job that earned them.
Permanent by design.  Rebirth Points and the boosts you buy with them are never wiped — only the job's level and Job Points are. Every cycle is a net gain in power; the only cost is the time to climb back.

Spending Rebirth Points

At the Rebirth altar, open Spend Rebirth Points to browse the boost catalog. Each category has a cost per level and a hard cap. Buy a level and the effect applies instantly — no relog needed. Cheaper stats (raw STR, HP, Accuracy) cost 1 per level; premium combat perks (Crit Dmg, WS Dmg, Haste, damage reduction) cost 3.

The Trial of Mastery

Every rebirth stacks a −15% EXP penalty on that job, capped at −90%. The penalty is per-job — it only slows the job that's been reborn, and vanishes the moment you switch to anything else. Re-leveling a six-times-reborn job is a true endgame grind; you always keep at least 10% of normal EXP, so the climb is always possible.

A System of Its Own

Rebirth is entirely separate from Ascension. It has its own currency, its own progress, and its own altar — they simply offer the same menu of stats. Because the two tracks are independent, their boosts stack: a stat you've maxed through Ascension can be raised again through Rebirth.

Where to Begin

✦

The Job Rebirth altar

Found at GM Home, alongside the Cross-Job Trainers. Speak to it on a job that's hit level 99 with maxed Job Points to begin — or visit any time to spend Rebirth Points you've already earned.

Designer notes — review

• Stacking caps: Rebirth currently inherits Ascension's per-category caps (e.g. +50 STR). As a separate track it can reach those caps again, so a fully-invested character could reach up to 2× a category (Ascension + Rebirth). Intended per the "separate system" brief — say the word if you'd prefer Rebirth to have its own (lower) caps.
• Deploy status: Lua is committed & pushed. Goes live after the pending C++ rebuild + restart — the only engine dependency is the resetJobPoints() binding that performs the Job Point wipe.
• Tunables (all in job_rebirth_catalog.lua): JP to qualify (2,100), Rebirth Points per rebirth (10), penalty per rebirth (15%) & cap (90%), max rebirths (uncapped), altar position.
