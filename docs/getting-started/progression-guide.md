# Progression Guide

This page maps the full the Relaunch server progression arc from a new character to endgame Prestige. If you haven't done setup yet, start with [First Steps](first-steps.md) — this guide picks up after your character is ready and you've made your first Hunting League kills.

---

## The Short Version

```
Setup → Hunting League (Rank I–V) → Reforge System
     → Augmenting → Infamy & Endgame → Prestige (infinite)
```

Every system on this server feeds marks into that path. The Hunting League is the spine; everything else branches off it.

---

## Stage 1 — Hunting League Rank I–III (Early)

**Goal:** Accumulate Hunt Marks and unlock Rank III.

The Hunting League is a ladder of NM-hunting ranks at [Escha ZiTah](../progression/index.md), from Initiate up to Legend. Each rank unlocks stronger NMs, better gear vendors, and higher marks-per-kill (5 per kill at Rank I, rising to 65 at Rank V). Your first priority is climbing ranks quickly so the marks-per-hour improves — Rank II unlocks at **150** lifetime marks and Rank III at **650**. The full rank list — each rank's NMs, marks-per-kill, and unlock cost — is in [The Ranks](../progression/index.md#the-ranks).

**What to buy early:** The Bronze gear tier (Beastmens Medals, 5 Hunt Marks each) from the [Gear Vendors](../progression/gear-vendors.md) at Escha ZiTah. Bronze pieces are inexpensive and cover the gear gap until you have Silver and Gold.

**Parallel tracks to start now:**

- **[Login Rewards](../progression/login-rewards.md)** — log in daily for +50 marks, hit 7/14/21/30-day streaks for bonuses.
- **[Daily Board](../progression/daily-board.md)** — the NPC at <!--npc:daily_board-->the Celennia Memorial Library<!--/npc--> (`!lib`) gives 3 daily objectives. Complete them for extra marks and a full-clear bonus.

## Stage 2 — Hunting League Rank IV–V (Mid)

**Goal:** Reach Rank V (Legend) and start farming 65-mark kills.

At Rank IV (**1,500** lifetime marks) you unlock King-class HNMs — Nidhogg, King Behemoth, Kirin. These are significantly harder than mid-tier NMs but drop much better and pay **38 marks** each. Party up if possible.

At **Rank V** (**3,000** lifetime marks) you're at the top of the Hunting League. Rank V NMs pay **65 marks** each (Shinryu pays 110) and your gear vendor unlocks Gold-tier items (Demons Medals). More importantly, **Prestige unlocks** — see Stage 6. Reaching Rank V is also an **Augment Tier key** — it opens the Tier 2 roll band at the Augment Moogle.

**What to buy at V:** Gold gear (Demons Medals, 40 Hunt Marks each) from the vendor. This is your gear floor while you pursue Reforge and Augments.

**Weekly Hunts:** Each week a fresh set of objectives rotates in (kill NMs, partied kills, speed kills, no-death streaks). The board is open at any rank, but each objective is gated by its own Hunting League rank requirement — the hardest need Rank V, so the full sweep is a Legend-tier feat. Sweeping all 5 in one week pays a **5,000 Hunt Marks** bonus. See [Weekly Hunt Board](../progression/weekly-hunts.md).

## Stage 3 — Reforge System (Mid to Late)

**Goal:** Earn AF, Relic, and Empyrean marks from their respective NM systems and build per-job gear sets.

The [Reforge System](../progression/reforge.md) runs on three separate mark currencies:

| Currency | NM family | The ladder |
|---|---|---|
| AF Marks | Sky Gods (Gwora-Corridor) | Genbu → Suzaku → Seiryu → Byakko → Kirin |
| Relic Marks | Unity NMs (Gwora-Corridor) | Bukhis → Khun → Padfoot → Glavoid → Tinnin |
| Empy Marks | Abyssea NMs (Gwora-Corridor) | Aello → Iratham → Briareus → Itzpapalotl → Hadhayosh |

Each track is a five-step difficulty ladder: entry NMs pay **25 marks** per kill and the apex NM of each track pays **150** — the highest marks-per-kill content on the server.

Spend marks at the Reforge NPCs in Gwora-Corridor to get job-specific AF/Relic/Empy gear sets at IL (item level) scaling, upgraded from base through +3 (+1 costs 300, +2 900, +3 2,000 of that set's marks per piece).

**Hunter's Guild builds here.** Killing Reforge NMs builds [Hunter's Guild](../progression/hunters-guild.md) reputation in the matching guild (AF / Relic / Empy). Higher guild rank means more marks per kill from that guild — it's a passive multiplier that snowballs over time.

## Stage 4 — Augmenting (Late)

**Goal:** Stamp custom augments onto your gear to push stats past their base caps.

The [Augment Moogle](../progression/augments.md) in <!--npc:augment_moogle-->Leafallia<!--/npc--> (`!leaf`) lets you trade catalyst items to apply custom stat augments to any piece of equipment. Hundreds of augment types are available across many stat families — Attack, Accuracy, Crit, TP Bonus, Cure Potency, and more. The full [catalyst → augment catalog](../progression/augments.md#catalyst-augment-catalog) lists every one.

Every augment line is **rolled**, and your roll band is gated by content: the **Augment Tier ladder** (Tiers 1–5) runs from your first custom NM kills to the endgame super-fights, and each tier's rolls sit strictly above the last. The [Augmenting guide](../progression/augmenting-guide.md) walks the whole ladder.

**The Augment Sage** sits next to the Moogle and adds two roll boosters:

- **Mastery ranks:** Unlock automatically at content milestones (Hunting League Rank, Prestige Level, Rebirths — nothing is consumed). Each rank raises your roll floor by +1 inside your tier's band and lifts your critical-augment chance from 5% to 30% — a crit makes every line in the trade land at the top of your band.
- **NM Affinities:** Kill specific Vana'diel NMs (Behemoth, King Behemoth, King Arthro, and more — 24 in all) for their unique trophies, then register each at the Augment Sage (Hunting League Rank 3 + 1,000 Hunt Marks per affinity) to **roll twice and keep the better** result in that stat category.

Stack a max-rank crit with an affinity at the top Augment Tier and a line writes its absolute cap — the Augment Archon ceiling.

See [Augment Sage](../progression/augment-sage.md) for the full rank requirements, NM list, and roll math.

## Stage 5 — Infamy & Endgame (Late)

**Goal:** Earn Infamy from endgame content and spend it at the Infamy Vendor.

**Infamy** is earned across the server's endgame — Abyssea NM hunts, scheduled Invasions, and the weekly Raid, among others. Spend it at the [Infamy Vendor](../progression/gear-vendors.md#infamy-vendor) in <!--npc:infamy_vendor-->Leafallia<!--/npc-->, which sells gear found nowhere else: relic-tier weapons, bard instruments, best-in-slot armor, and per-job +4 Reforge Sets.

## Stage 6 — Prestige / Ascension (Endgame · Infinite)

**Goal:** Unlock Prestige at Rank V, clear the Nightmare Court, and accumulate per-job Ascension Points.

[Prestige](../progression/prestige.md) is an infinite per-job progression layer that unlocks the moment you reach Hunting League **Tier V (Legend)**. Travel to the Ascension Altar in Provenance (`!provenance`) to begin.

**Each ascension cycle:**

1. **Clear the Nightmare Court** — three superbosses summoned one at a time at the Altar: **Diabolos, the Dream Devourer** · **Medusa, the Gorgon Queen** · **Odin, the Doombringer**. All three must be killed on every cycle; kills don't carry over (and the Court's roster deepens as your Prestige Level climbs).
2. **Pay the Mark cost** — it starts at 500 Hunt Marks, rises by 500 with each ascension, and plateaus at a 3,000 cap (see the [Ascension Economy](../progression/prestige.md#ascension-economy)).
3. **Earn Ascension Points (AP)** for your current main job.

**Spend AP** at the Altar on permanent per-job stat boosts: base stats, combat traits, magic support, mitigation, utility. Every stat has a level cap, and there's a wide spread of purchasable categories — see the [AP Spend Table](../progression/prestige.md#ap-spend-table). With infinite ascensions at the capped cost each, this is the true long-term chase.

## Quick Reference — Marks Economy

Marks flow in from every system on this page, and a couple of sinks pull them back out. Rather than restate the numbers here (where they could drift), each lives on its owning page:

- **Hunting League kills** scale up with rank (5 → 65 marks) — see [The Ranks](../progression/index.md#the-ranks).
- **Reforge NM kills** climb a per-track ladder (25 → 150 marks) — the top NMs are the highest marks-per-kill content — see [NM pools and rewards](../progression/reforge.md#nm-pools-and-rewards).
- **Daily login + streak milestones** — see [Login Rewards](../progression/login-rewards.md).
- **Daily Board** and **Weekly Hunt Board** sweeps — see [Daily Board](../progression/daily-board.md) and [Weekly Hunt Board](../progression/weekly-hunts.md).
- **Sinks:** the Hunting League reward shop, rank unlocks, and each Prestige ascension (see the [Ascension Economy](../progression/prestige.md#ascension-economy)).

On top of all of it, your **[Hunter's Guild](../progression/hunters-guild.md)** rank multiplies every kill's marks — at Grandmaster a Legend kill pays roughly double, and the Apex Hunter capstone pushes it higher still. The guild track is a long grind but the payoff is real.
