# Chocobo Derby

In the **Purgonorgo Isle** (`!hub`), the **Race Caller** runs the races. Put gil on a runner and
watch it play out — and if you've raised a chocobo of your own that's tough
enough, line it up at the gate and bet on yourself.

!!! tip "Summary"
    Bet gil on chocobo races at the Race Caller on Purgonorgo Isle (`!hub`); raise a strong
    enough chocobo of your own and you can enter it as a runner for a bigger
    payout.

## Where to play

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="chocobo-derby-access" -->
The **Race Caller** stands in **Purgonorgo Isle** (`!hub`). Step up to check the odds board, place a bet, and watch the race play out.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="chocobo-derby-access" -->

## Placing a bet

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="chocobo-derby-betting" -->
**Stakes:** 10,000 / 50,000 / 250,000 gil.

Each runner carries odds set by its hidden strength: the long-shots pay the most, the favourites the least. The house keeps about **15%** of the fair payout as its cut, and even a heavy favourite always returns at least **1.15×** your stake when it wins. Back the wrong bird and you lose your bet.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="chocobo-derby-betting" -->

## The field

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="chocobo-derby-field" -->
Every race fields **6 runners**. If you aren't racing a chocobo of your own, they're all from the house stable; if you are, your bird takes one of the gates. The stable regulars are drawn from this roster:

- Chocofury · Bolt v. Kweh · Lady Bolt
- Sir Scratch · Meteor Mike · Yellowcomet
- Dust Dan · Sandy Sally · Gallop Gus
- Wark Wonder · Featherluck · Kwehsar

A race runs about **20 seconds** of live commentary before the winner crosses the line.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="chocobo-derby-field" -->

## Racing your own chocobo

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="chocobo-derby-ownbird" -->
Raised a chocobo of your own? Once it reaches the **adolescent** stage (or older) you can enter it as a runner instead of just betting on the stable.

Your bird races on its real ability — its strength, endurance, and how much affection you've raised it with all feed its hidden racing form, so a well-loved, well-trained chocobo genuinely out-classes the house runners and races at shorter odds.

And there's a reason to root for your own: winning a race on **your** chocobo pays an extra **25%** on top of the normal payout.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="chocobo-derby-ownbird" -->

Like the casino, the derby is a long-run gil sink — the house keeps its cut
over time — but a well-judged bet, or a champion bird of your own, can still pay
off big on the day.
