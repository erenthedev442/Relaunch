# The Star-Devourer — Weekly Raid Boss

The ultimate test on Legendary: a colossal, multi-phase raid boss lurking at the Voidgate in Escha-RuAun. The Star-Devourer is a server-shared fight anyone can join, but its richest rewards are locked to once per week — so rally a raid, learn the dance, and bring it down.

!!! tip "Summary"
    A weekly-lockout, multi-phase raid boss (The Star-Devourer) at Escha-RuAun — survive the stance dance, the tendril adds, the dispel sweep, and the enrage for marks and Infamy once a week.

## Starting the fight

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="star-devourer-access" -->
Seek out the **Voidgate Sentinel** in **Escha-RuAun**, standing beside the Voidspire Warden. Speak to them to summon **The Star-Devourer** — the fight is server-shared, so anyone in the area can join the brawl. It's a true raid: bring a crowd.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="star-devourer-access" -->

## The boss

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="star-devourer-scale" -->
**The Star-Devourer** is a **level 200** behemoth with a colossal health pool — roughly **45× a normal boss's HP**. This is a long, phase-driven endurance fight, not a quick burn — expect to work through every stage below.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="star-devourer-scale" -->

## Fight mechanics

The Star-Devourer is a scripted encounter — its phases fire in a fixed order as you chip away at its health. Knowing what's coming is the difference between a clean kill and a wipe.

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="star-devourer-mechanics" -->
**The stance dance — from 90% HP.** Once the boss drops to 90% it begins alternating between two stances roughly every **45 seconds**:

- **Aegis Stance** — blades glance off (near-immune to physical), but magic rips through.
- **Umbral Stance** — spells gutter out (near-immune to magic), but steel bites deep.

Watch the on-screen call-out and switch your damage type to match, or your numbers will vanish.

**The tendrils — at 75% and 30% HP.** The Devourer spawns **Starspawn Tendrils** and starts rapidly healing itself while they live — the tendrils feed the maw. Cut them down fast to stop the heal and resume your assault.

**Dispel sweep — at 50% HP.** A wave of unbeing strips protective buffs from everyone engaged. Be ready to re-buff and press on.

**Final fury — at 15% HP.** The boss permanently spikes its attack and attack speed for the last stretch — the closing burn is a dangerous one.

**Soft enrage — 12 minutes in.** If the fight drags on this long, a massive permanent buff lands and the encounter becomes a race. Kill it before the clock turns the Devourer untouchable.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="star-devourer-mechanics" -->

If every living raider leaves the boss's area for too long, the fight resets and the Devourer despawns — keep someone on it.

## Rewards

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="star-devourer-rewards" -->
Rewards are on a **weekly lockout** (resetting Monday 00:00 UTC):

- **First kill of the week:** 2,500 Hunt Marks + 500 Infamy.
- **Repeat kills the same week:** 250 Hunt Marks as a consolation, so it's always worth helping your friends down it again.

You must be near the boss when it dies to get credit.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="star-devourer-rewards" -->

The weekly reset shares the same Monday 00:00 UTC anchor as the Weekly Hunt Board and the weekly Raid, so you can plan your big-ticket weekly runs together.
