# Omen

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="omen-intro" -->
The chessboard awaits. **Omen** is the Reisenjima Henge gauntlet from the November 2016 era of retail: five gates of trials, three Glassy sentinels, and the **Caturae** — Kin, Gin, Fu, Kyou and Kei — with the hidden Prime, **Ou**, beyond them. It is the home of **paragon job cards**, **Caturae scales** and the **Regal** treasures, and the road to **Artifact armor +2 and +3**.

!!! tip "Quick start"
    Get **Phoenix's blessing** and a **mystical canteen** from **Incantrix** at     Ethereal Ingress #10 in Reisenjima, gather your group (up to 18),     and choose *Enter Omen*. You start with **10 minutes**; each gate     cleared grants **+10** (cap **50**).
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="omen-intro" -->

## Getting in

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="omen-entry" -->
**Incantrix** and **Coelestrox** stand at **Ethereal Ingress #10** in Reisenjima (reachable through the Ethereal Ingress network).

- **Phoenix's blessing** — permanent key item; Incantrix bestows it on any level-99 adventurer.
- **Mystical canteen** — one per entrant, consumed on entry. Incantrix fills one every **20 hours** and banks up to **3** for you while you're away.
- **Group size** — solo up to a full alliance of **18**. Only the leader initiates; everyone needs their own canteen and must be standing nearby.
- **Time** — **10 minutes** on entry, **+10** per ethereal ingress used, **+30** for the smaller light, hard cap **50 minutes**.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="omen-entry" -->

## The five gates

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="omen-floors" -->
| Gate | What awaits | Bonus objectives |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — First Gate** | Tiger, Fly — 14 Sweetwater + 2 Transcended, plus a random floor objective | 3 in 3 min |
| **2 — Second Gate** | Beetle, Leech — 14 Sweetwater + 2 Transcended, plus a random floor objective ← *larger/smaller light choice* | 3 in 3 min |
| **3 — Third Gate** | One of the three **Glassy sentinels**, at random | — |
| **4 — Fourth Gate** | By mid-boss route (Craver: Skeleton, Cyhiraeth, Ghost, Doll, Gorger: Chapuli, Treant, Mantis, Doomed, Thinker: Elemental, Slime, Lucani, Worm) — 28 Sweetwater + 4 Transcended, plus a random floor objective | 5 in 5 min |
| **5 — Final Gate** | The **Caturae of your choice** (picked at the fourth gate's ingress) | — |

**The smaller light — Luminous Rampart.** Choosing the *smaller* light at the second gate grants **+30 minutes** and replaces the remaining gates with one sprawling trial: **56 creatures** (Rabbit, Mandragora, Lizard, Vulture, Ladybug, Porxie, Panopt, Unseelie) in successive waves, with **10 bonus objectives** over 10 minutes. Fell every last one — with the run's opener alive at the final blow — and the exit ingress offers something else entirely (see **Ou** below).

**Hidden vault.** Leaving the third or final gate has a **10%** chance to detour through a chamber of treasure caskets: gil, paragon cards, moonbow materials, scales, a spare canteen — and sometimes <a class="item-link" href="https://www.ffxiah.com/item/28273" data-img="https://www.bg-wiki.com/images/a/a2/Regal_Pumps_description.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Regal Pumps</a>.

## Objectives & paragon cards

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="omen-objectives" -->
Each trash gate rolls **one main objective** at random:

- Vanquish one specific monster.
- Vanquish N Sweetwater monsters.
- Vanquish all Transcended monsters.
- Vanquish all monsters.
- No trial this time. The ingress stands open.

Alongside it, timed **bonus objectives** begin the moment battle is joined (N scales with group size, capped at 6):

- Vanquish a number of foes
- Use a number of job abilities on your foes
- Cast a number of spells on your foes
- Execute a number of skillchains
- Perform a number of magic bursts
- Deal a number of critical hits
- Use a number of elemental weapon skills
- Use a number of physical weapon skills
- Use a number of weapon skills
- Deal 2,000+ damage in a single attack
- Deal 30,000+ damage with a single weapon skill
- Deal 30,000+ damage with a single magic burst
- Deal 15,000+ damage with a single (non-burst) magic attack

Every **5 objectives** completed (main objectives, bonus objectives and boss kills all count — progress carries between runs) pays every member **one paragon card for their current main job**. Coelestrox converts **5 cards of one job into 1 of another**.

## Differences from retail

The implementation follows [bg-wiki's Omen reference](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Category:Omen)
wherever the data exists. Known deviations, kept deliberately small:

- **One platform, five gates.** Every floor stages on the retail first-floor
  platform of Reisenjima Henge; the ethereal ingress advances the run in
  place instead of teleporting between rooms (the other floors' geometry was
  never captured on any server emulator).
- **Stand-in boss models.** The Caturae's true model IDs were never publicly
  captured, so the five wear Escha Zdei arcana forms and Ou appears as a
  battleclad chariot. Their kits and drops are unaffected.
- **Signature moves are scripted.** Moves whose animations were never
  captured (Zero Hour, Dancing Fullers, Ebullient Nullification, Unfaltering
  Bravado, Target/Eleventh Dimension, Prophylaxis, the Glassy Sync moves)
  resolve as scripted effects with chat telegraphs instead of client
  animations.
- **Phoenix's blessing is granted by Incantrix** at level 99 instead of
  requiring the full Rhapsodies of Vana'diel mission line.
- **Reforging completes instantly** at the retail "cheap"-tier fee (paid in
  Escha Beads), and the +3 step's second-day crafted materials are waived.
- The **"restore 500 HP ten times"** bonus objective is not offered; all
  other retail objective types are implemented.
