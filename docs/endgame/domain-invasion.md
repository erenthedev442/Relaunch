# Domain Invasion

Domain Invasion is a server-wide cooperative event that fires eight times a day across two Escha zones. Dahaks flood Escha - Zi'Tah, Lamiae flood Escha - Ru'Aun — and each assault ends with a powerful named NM that has to go down before the clock runs out.

!!! tip "Summary"
    Two-wave events in the Escha zones (Zi'Tah / Ru'Aun alternating every 3 hours, eight times a day). Kill the vanguard, then kill the boss. Rewards: Escha Silt, Escha Beads, and Domain Points (daily cap 80). Type `!diwarp` to warp to the active zone.

## Schedule

Domain Invasion fires **8 times per day** (every 3 hours), alternating between the two Escha zones:

| UTC time | Zone |
|---:|---|
| 00:00 UTC | **Escha - Zi'Tah** |
| 03:00 UTC | **Escha - Ru'Aun** |
| 06:00 UTC | **Escha - Zi'Tah** |
| 09:00 UTC | **Escha - Ru'Aun** |
| 12:00 UTC | **Escha - Zi'Tah** |
| 15:00 UTC | **Escha - Ru'Aun** |
| 18:00 UTC | **Escha - Zi'Tah** |
| 21:00 UTC | **Escha - Ru'Aun** |

A server-wide warning broadcasts **5 minutes** before each window. The event only fires if at least one player is in the zone when the window opens — it waits up to **10 minutes** for a defender. Type `!diwarp` to teleport straight to the active zone.

## The Assault

Two waves hit each zone in sequence. The first is a vanguard of trash mobs; the second brings a smaller group of adds alongside the zone's named boss. The bosses use the hardcore mechanics library — expect stance shifts, AoE attacks, HP-phase mechanics, and a berserk timer.

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="di-zones" -->
### Escha - Zi'Tah

| Wave | Enemy level |
|---|---:|
| **Dahak Vanguard** | 120 |
| **Azi Dahaka** | 140 |
| **Azi Dahaka** (boss) | 150 |

### Escha - Ru'Aun

| Wave | Enemy level |
|---|---:|
| **Lamia Vanguard** | 120 |
| **Naga Raja** | 140 |
| **Naga Raja** (boss) | 150 |

Each assault is two waves: a vanguard of trash mobs followed by a boss wave that brings adds alongside the named NM. Wave count scales with attendance — more defenders means more enemies per wave. Clear both waves within **10 minutes** to claim victory.

## Rewards

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="di-rewards" -->
Rewards go to every player in the zone at the relevant moment. Domain Points are capped per UTC day — you can keep earning Silt and Beads after the cap, but no more Domain Points until the next day.

| Outcome | Escha Silt | Escha Beads | Domain Points |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Wave clear** (per wave) | 75 | — | — |
| **Victory** (all waves + boss killed) | 150 | 3 | 30 |
| **Time expired** (consolation) | 50 | 1 | 10 |

**Daily Domain Point cap: 80 per character.** On a perfect run (victory in both zones per day) you would earn 120 — well above the cap, so 80 is the real ceiling. Spend Domain Points at the Domain Invasion exchange NPC in the hub.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="di-rewards" -->

## Domain Points exchange

Domain Points spend at the exchange NPC in the hub. Check the NPC's menu for the current stock — the catalog drives both the event rewards and the shop, so the numbers on this page are always current.

## Tips

- **Attendance scales the wave.** More defenders = more enemies per wave, but also more people splitting boss aggro and burning it down faster. Show up even with a small group.
- **!diwarp any time.** It warps to the active zone if one is running, or to Zi'Tah by default.
- **Wave silt is shared broadly.** Anyone in the zone at wave-clear time earns silt — you don't need to have been there from wave 1.
- **Victory vs consolation.** You need the boss dead before time expires for the full payout. Partial clears (wave 1 only) still give per-wave silt but the consolation Domain Points on timeout are smaller than the victory payout.
