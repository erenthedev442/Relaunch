# Casino — Lady Luck

Feeling lucky? **Lady Luck** runs a four-game casino in **Purgonorgo Isle** where you can gamble your gil on slots, high-low, roulette, and dice. Every game keeps a small house edge, so over time it's a gil sink — but a hot streak can pay out huge, and any single win big enough is shouted to the whole server.

## The games

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="casino-games" -->
| Game | How it works | Payouts | House edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Slots** | Spin three reels, match three symbols | 7×3 = 150×, Bell×3 = 30×, Melon×3 = 10×, Cherry×3 = 4× | ~8.4% |
| **High-Low** | A card 1–13 is shown; bet whether the next is higher or lower | Odds-based — safe calls pay barely over 1×, long shots pay big | ~8.0% |
| **Roulette** | Single-zero wheel (0–36); bet a colour/odd/even or a single number | Even-money **2×**, single number **35×** | ~2.7% even / ~5.4% single |
| **Dice** | Roll 2d6; bet the **High (8–12)** / **Low (2–6)** band or **Lucky 7** | High/Low **2.25×**, Lucky 7 **5×** | ~6.2% band / ~16.7% on 7 |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="casino-games" -->

All payouts are **total-return** multipliers: a 2× win on a 25,000 gil bet returns 50,000 (a net gain of 25,000), a push returns your bet, and a loss keeps it. The house edge is the long-run cost of playing — small on the even-money bets, bigger on the long-shots that pay the most.
