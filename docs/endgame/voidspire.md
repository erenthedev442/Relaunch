# The Voidspire

A spire bored down into a sealed nightmare, and now it never ends. The
**Voidspire** is an endless, floor-by-floor gauntlet — descend as far as you
can, and your deepest floor is your score on the leaderboard. A single wipe
ends the run, so press your luck.

!!! tip "Summary"
    An endless descent at the Warden in Escha-RuAun — clear floor after floor, your
    deepest floor is your leaderboard score, and one wipe ends the run.

## Where to start

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="voidspire-access" -->
Find the **Warden** in **Escha-RuAun** and choose to descend. The run begins after a short breather, then the floors come one after another — clear a floor and the next spawns automatically. A single wipe ends the run and locks in your deepest floor.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="voidspire-access" -->

## How the floors scale

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="voidspire-scaling" -->
Every floor is tougher than the last. Three things climb as you descend:

- **Monster level** starts around **120** and rises until it tops out near **205**.
- **Monster HP** begins at roughly **4×** the normal amount and swells up to **30×** the deeper you go.
- **Pack size** grows from **1** monster, adding one more every **10** floors, up to **5** at once.

Beyond level, the real bite comes from the monsters' offense — attack, accuracy and attack speed ramp hard, so deep floors overwhelm you with incoming damage rather than turning into unkillable sponges.

The run moves through four difficulty bands as you descend:

| Floors | Difficulty |
|---|---|
| 1–9 | **Easy** |
| 10–24 | **Normal** |
| 25–44 | **Hard** |
| 45+ | **Insane** |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="voidspire-scaling" -->

## Affixes

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="voidspire-affixes" -->
Starting at **floor 10**, the Void layers extra modifiers onto every monster. One more affix switches on every **15** floors, up to **5** at once. The set is rolled fresh for each run and shown to you when you begin, so no two descents play quite the same.

| Affix | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ravenous** | They regenerate from the Void itself — sustain your damage. |
| **Cruel** | Their blows fall like falling stars. |
| **Manic** | A frenzy of impossible speed. |
| **Unerring** | Nothing escapes their sight — evasion is useless here. |
| **Warded** | A void-shell blunts every blow. |
| **Venomous** | Their touch corrodes flesh and magic alike. |
| **Phantasmal** | Half-dreamed, and maddeningly slippery. |
| **Colossal** | Bloated with stolen life. |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="voidspire-affixes" -->

## Rewards

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="voidspire-rewards" -->
Clearing a floor pays **5 + 2 × the floor number** in marks, so deeper floors are worth more each. The Voidspire's real prize, though, is the leaderboard — your deepest floor is your score.

Reaching certain depths for the first time pays a one-time bonus on top, announced server-wide:

| Reach floor | Bonus marks |
|---:|---:|
| 10 | **2,500** |
| 25 | **10,000** |
| 50 | **25,000** |
| 75 | **40,000** |
| 100 | **70,000** |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="voidspire-rewards" -->

Marks spend across the same Hunting League economy as the rest of your
progression. Climb carefully — there is no checkpoint, and the leaderboard only
remembers how deep you dared to go.
