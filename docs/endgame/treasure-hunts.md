# Treasure Hunts

Out in the field, a Hunting League kill might turn up a tattered map with an X
on it. Carry it to the right corner of the world, dig where it's warm, and pry
open a buried strongbox full of marks, gil, and augment catalysts.

!!! tip "Summary"
    Hunting League kills can drop treasure maps; take a map to its overworld
    zone and dig — hot/cold feedback guides you to a buried strongbox of marks,
    gil, and augment catalysts.

## Finding maps

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="treasure-hunts-drops" -->
Every Hunting League kill has a chance to shake loose a treasure map — **8%** at the lowest hunt tier, rising **2%** per tier (so a top-tier kill is around **16%**). You can only carry one map at a time, so dig up the one you have before going for another.

Tougher hunts drop better maps:

| Map | Drops from |
|---|---|
| **Tattered Treasure Map** | the first–second hunt tiers |
| **Weathered Treasure Map** | the third–fourth hunt tiers |
| **Pristine Treasure Map** | the fifth hunt tier |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="treasure-hunts-drops" -->

## The dig

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="treasure-hunts-dig" -->
Your **first dig** buries the strongbox somewhere nearby on solid ground. Every dig after that gives you a temperature reading and a compass nudge — follow the heat until you are standing on top of it:

- *SCORCHING! It must be right here!*
- *Hot! Very close now.*
- *Warm. Getting closer.*
- *Cool... a fair walk yet.*
- *Freezing cold. The mark is far away.*

Dig within about **12 fulms** of the box and it's yours.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="treasure-hunts-dig" -->

## What's in the box

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="treasure-hunts-loot" -->
What's inside scales with the map's tier:

| Map | Marks | Gil | Augment catalysts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Tattered Treasure Map** | 250 | 25,000 | 1 |
| **Weathered Treasure Map** | 600 | 75,000 | 2 |
| **Pristine Treasure Map** | 1,500 | 200,000 | 4 |

Cracking open a **Pristine Treasure Map** is announced server-wide — the whole world hears about your haul.

Catalysts are the same ones used to power up augmented gear, so the world tour keeps the augment economy fed.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="treasure-hunts-loot" -->

Keep an empty inventory slot or two before you dig — a strongbox that can't fit
all its catalysts pays you marks for the overflow instead.
