# Scheduled Invasions — Defend GM Home

Twice a day the Voidsent pour into GM Home, the server's social hub, and it falls to whoever's around to throw them back. Invasions are a Besieged-style outpost defense: escalating waves of enemies that grow with the size of your defending force, capped off by a boss. Survive the assault and every defender shares in the spoils.

!!! tip "Summary"
    A twice-daily Besieged-style defense of GM Home — waves of Voidsent that scale with how many defenders show up, ending in a boss, rewarding marks and Infamy to all who hold the line.

## When it happens

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="invasions-schedule" -->
The Voidsent strike **8 times a day**, at **00:00 UTC and 03:00 UTC and 06:00 UTC and 09:00 UTC and 12:00 UTC and 15:00 UTC and 18:00 UTC and 21:00 UTC**.

- A server-wide warning shouts **5 minutes** before each window so you can rally at GM Home.
- The assault only fires if **at least one defender** is standing in GM Home when the window opens — it stays armed for up to **10 minutes** waiting for someone to show. No defenders, no invasion.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="invasions-schedule" -->

Just be in GM Home when a window opens and you're automatically a defender — there's nothing to sign up for.

## The waves

The assault rolls in as a ladder of escalating waves, each tougher than the last, before the Voidsent commander itself appears:

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="invasions-waves" -->
| Wave | Enemy level |
|---|---:|
| **Vanguard** | 110 |
| **Shock Troops** | 125 |
| **Dreadguard** | 140 |
| **Apex Guard** | 155 |
| **The Warlord** | 165 |
| **Voidsent Warlord** (boss) | 175 |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="invasions-waves" -->

The final wave brings its own trash alongside the boss — clear them all and the boss to win.

## Scaling and time limit

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="invasions-scaling" -->
The more defenders who answer the call, the **larger each wave** — bring friends and the horde swells to match. The whole assault, from the first wave to the boss, must be cleared within **15 minutes**. Hold the line and fell the boss before the clock runs out to claim victory.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="invasions-scaling" -->

## Rewards

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="invasions-rewards" -->
Every defender present is paid out — credit goes to anyone in GM Home at the relevant moment:

- **150 Hunt Marks** for each wave cleared.
- **1,125 Hunt Marks + 100 Infamy** on a full victory (the boss falls). This is one of the few sources of Infamy outside the dungeons.
- **150 Hunt Marks** as a consolation if the clock beats the defense.

Victory also drops gear-vendor **medals** — a guaranteed Kindreds Medal haul, plus a rare **~1-in-1** chance at a Demons Medal for the very best gear.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="invasions-rewards" -->

Those medals feed the Armor and Weapons gear vendors (see the `!hunt` gear loop), so a strong turnout at invasions is a steady route to better equipment.
