# Provisioners' League

A non-combat league for anglers and crafters. The **League Steward** at Reisenjima Henge tracks your fishing weigh-ins and high-quality craft turn-ins, awarding **League Points** that climb a five-rank ladder — and every rank-up permanently boosts the marks you earn around the server.

!!! tip "Summary"
    Fish and turn in HQ crafts at the League Steward in Reisenjima Henge to earn League Points; climbing the five-rank ladder grants a permanent, stacking mark bonus.

## Where to join

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="provisioners-league-access" -->
Seek out the **League Steward** at **Reisenjima Henge** — he keeps the books for both anglers and crafters. Talk to him to check your rank, your League Point total, and this week's featured fish.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="provisioners-league-access" -->

## Earning League Points

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="provisioners-league-scoring" -->
There are two ways to earn **League Points**:

**Fishing weigh-ins** — every fish you catch is worth points that scale with the fish's skill: the tougher the catch, the more it pays. On top of that:

- A **big fish** (the ruler-measured monsters) is worth **2×** points.
- A **legendary** catch is league gold — worth **10×** points.
- This week's **featured fish** pays a **3×** bonus — a rotating target that keeps the weekly race fresh and is always something a normal angler can land.

**Crafting turn-ins** — every high-quality (HQ) item you hand in at the server's Crafting Exchange also credits League Points alongside its usual reward, so your crafting progress counts here too.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="provisioners-league-scoring" -->

There's also a **weekly leaderboard** scored on the points you earn that week — a fresh race every week, separate from your lifetime total.

## The rank ladder

Your **lifetime** League Points decide your rank. Each rank you reach grants a permanent mark bonus that **stacks** as you climb — reaching a higher rank keeps every bonus below it.

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="provisioners-league-ranks" -->
| Rank | League Points | Mark bonus |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Apprentice Provisioner** | — | — |
| **Journeyman Provisioner** | 750 | +250 |
| **Expert Provisioner** | 2,500 | +750 |
| **Master Provisioner** | 7,500 | +2,000 |
| **Legendary Provisioner** | 20,000 | +5,000 |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="provisioners-league-ranks" -->
