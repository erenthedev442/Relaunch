# Dynamis (Classic)

The original ten Dynamis zones — the four cities, Beaucedine, Xarcabard, and
the four Dreamlands — are live on relaunch as the home of **Dynamis currency,
reforge materials, and the Attestation NM chain**.

## What drops here

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="dynamis-classic-overview" -->
On relaunch, classic Dynamis drops **currency and reforge materials only** — all retail gear drops are removed (the [Reforge System](../progression/reforge.md) is the gear source, fed by the medals and materials these zones drop).

The exception worth planning a run around is the **Attestation NM chain in Dynamis-Beaucedine**:

1. Kill **Hydra Corps Fomors** — each job drops one of five *Fortune-telling parchments* (5%, Rare).
2. Trade the parchment to its **???** spot to force-pop the matching **Fomor NM**.
3. The NM drops **exactly one Attestation per kill** (even split across its set).

These are the **same Attestations the [Aeonic weapon](../progression/aeonic-weapons.md) forge consumes** (14 of one type per weapon) — so Dynamis-Beaucedine is a full alternative to farming [Geas Fete](geas-fete.md) bosses for them.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="dynamis-classic-overview" -->
