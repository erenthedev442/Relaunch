# The Endless Tower

A spire with no roof, only more floors. The **Endless Tower** is a solo
gauntlet that climbs through ever-stronger monsters, a boss waiting on every
tenth floor. Fight your way to the top and the Pinnacle Sovereign falls — and
with it, a piece of your Prime Weapon legend. One death, and the run is over.

!!! tip "Summary"
    A solo 50-floor climb from the Endless Tower Arbiter in GM Home — a boss
    every 10 floors, no Trusts allowed, one death ends the run, and reaching
    floor 50 completes Prime Weapon Trial 2.

## Where to start

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="endless-tower-access" -->
Speak to the **Endless Tower Arbiter** in **GM Home** and choose *Enter the Tower* to be warped into **Walk Of Echoes**. After a short breather the first floor spawns, and each cleared floor leads straight into the next.

This is a **solo** gauntlet — Trusts are disabled inside the Tower. A single death ends the run on the spot with no reward and no floor saved, so you only ever advance by surviving. You can bail out early at any time with **`!tower abort`**, and **`!tower`** on its own reports your current floor (or your best-ever floor when you're not in a run).
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="endless-tower-access" -->

## How the floors scale

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="endless-tower-floors" -->
The Tower is **50 floors** tall. Every floor throws a pack of monsters at you; clear them all and the next floor spawns after a brief pause. Monsters get a fresh, larger level the deeper you climb, and the pack grows from **3** monsters on the lowest floors toward a bigger crowd near the top.

Floors are grouped into ten-floor bands, each a sharp step up in monster level:

| Floors | Monster level |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | **120** |
| 11–20 | **155** |
| 21–30 | **185** |
| 31–40 | **215** |
| 41–50 | **240** |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="endless-tower-floors" -->

## Bosses

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="endless-tower-bosses" -->
Every **10th floor** is a boss floor: a single towering monster stands between you and the next band. Each boss rolls **one random affix** when it appears, so the same boss can play very differently from run to run.

The bosses, band by band:

| Floor | Boss | Level | HP |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 10 | **Guardian of the Spire** | 135 | 600,000 |
| 20 | **Shadow of the Abyss** | 170 | 1,200,000 |
| 30 | **The Eternal Warden** | 205 | 2,500,000 |
| 40 | **Lord of the Void** | 240 | 4,000,000 |
| 50 | **The Pinnacle Sovereign** | 275 | 7,000,000 |

Possible boss affixes:

- **Fortified**
- **Frenzied**
- **Regenerating**
- **Empowered**
- **Colossal**
- **Furious**
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="endless-tower-bosses" -->

## Rewards

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="endless-tower-rewards" -->
The Tower's prize is the climb itself. Reaching **floor 50** and defeating **The Pinnacle Sovereign** completes **Prime Weapon Trial 2** — the gateway to forging your Prime Weapon at the Prime Armory. The trial only needs to be cleared once; after that, floor 50 is pure bragging rights.

Your **deepest floor ever cleared** is tracked as a personal best, so even a run that ends short of the top still pushes your record on the leaderboard. There are no checkpoints — the Tower only remembers how high you climbed.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="endless-tower-rewards" -->

Climb carefully — there is no checkpoint, no party to lean on, and the Tower
only remembers how high you dared to go.
