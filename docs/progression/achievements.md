# Achievement System

Achievements are personal milestones that award bonus **Hunt Marks** and occasionally an **in-game title** when you hit them for the first time. Every eligible player can earn each achievement — they are not server-first exclusives.

When you complete an achievement, a message appears in your system log showing the milestone name, its description, and how many marks you received. Server-wide broadcasts are sent for the highest-tier achievements so the whole server can celebrate.

## Hunting Milestones

Trigger based on your total Hunting League NM kill count.

| Milestone | Trigger | Reward | Title Unlock |
|---|---|---:|---|
| First Hunt | 1st HL kill | +50 | — |
| Ten Hunts In | 10th HL kill | +100 | — |
| Centennial Hunter | 100th HL kill | +300 | Desert Hunter |
| Legendary Slayer | 1,000th HL kill | +1,000 | Hero Among Heroes |

## Lifetime Marks

Trigger when your cumulative earned Hunt Marks cross a threshold (spending marks does not reduce the lifetime total).

| Milestone | Trigger | Reward | Title Unlock |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Mark of 1,000 | 1,000 lifetime marks | +200 | — |
| Mark of 10,000 | 10,000 lifetime marks | +500 | — |
| Mark of 100,000 | 100,000 lifetime marks | +2,000 | Paragon of Warrior Excellence |

## Notes

- Each achievement can only be earned **once per character**.
- Titles are unlocked for your collection — use the `/title` command to display one.
- Mark bonuses are added to your current Hunt Marks balance immediately.
- You can track your progress through the `!achievements` command in-game (lists which milestones you have already earned).
