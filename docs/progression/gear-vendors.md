# Gear Vendors

![Anvil and coins](../assets/emblems/gear-vendors.svg){ .lgnd-emblem }

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="vendors-intro" -->
Three custom NPCs at **Escha ZiTah** sell endgame gear for currency you'll already be earning from regular content. All three stand on the same row next to the [Hunting League](index.md) NPCs in the same zone — one trip covers your full progression loop.

| NPC | Location (Escha ZiTah) | Sells |
|---|---|---|
| **Armor Vendor** | `(-3, -0.5, -30)` | Head / Body / Hands / Legs / Feet |
| **Weapons Vendor** | `(-6, -0.5, -30)` | Every weapon category, all jobs |
| **Accessories Vendor** | `(-9, -0.5, -30)` | Neck / Earrings / Rings / Back / Waist |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="vendors-intro" -->

## Currencies

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="currencies" -->
Both vendors use the same three currencies, tier-gated:

| Tier | Currency | How to get |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | Beastmens Medal | 5 Hunt Marks at the Hunting League Seals NPC |
| **Silver** | Kindreds Medal | 15 Hunt Marks at the Hunting League Seals NPC |
| **Gold** | Demons Medal | 40 Hunt Marks at the Hunting League Seals NPC |

!!! tip "Buy seals in bulk"
    The Seals NPC supports multi-quantity purchases — when previewing a seal, you'll see **Buy 1**, **Buy 10**, and **Buy max** options. The max is computed live from your mark balance and is capped at **99 per click** (one full inventory stack). Click again to grab another stack.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="currencies" -->

## Accessory NPC (medal-paid jewelry)

A third member of the Escha ZiTah gear-vendor row, paid in the **same Bronze/Silver/Gold medal economy** as the Armor and Weapons NPCs. Covers all 5 jewelry/accessory slots — **Neck, Waist, Earring, Ring, Back**. Items are auto-selected by `tools/score_accessories.py` from the live item DB using the same role-balanced scoring model that powers the Armor NPC, so a "DD score of 80" means the same thing on a head piece, an axe, and a ring.

Re-run `tools/rebalance_all.bat` after any DB or scoring-weight change — that one batch refreshes Armor + Weapons + Accessory catalogs together.

## Infamy Vendor

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="infamy-vendor" -->
The **Infamy Vendor** stands at <!--npc:infamy_vendor-->Purgonorgo Isle<!--/npc--> (`!hub`) and is paid in **Infamy**, earned from endgame content -- Abyssea NM hunts, Invasions, and the weekly Raid. It is a hand-curated **accessory shop** -- best-in-slot neck, ear, ring, waist, and back pieces. Weapons and armor are no longer sold here; they drop from the [Voidwatch](../endgame/voidwatch.md) NMs instead. All costs below are in Infamy.

Looking for **+4 armor**? That's no longer bought here — the +4 tier is an earned upgrade at the [Dynamis-Divergence Forge](../endgame/dynamis-divergence.md) (trade a reforged +3 AF/Relic piece plus [D] materials). The Infamy Vendor sells accessories only.
