# Boom (Custom Job)

**Boom** is a custom job on this server — a staff-swinging melee/hybrid DD whose elemental spells randomly **detonate** for massive bonus damage. No pets. No avatars. Just you, a staff, and a very satisfying explosion.

!!! warning "Relaunch exclusive"
    Boom occupies the Summoner job slot. The client still shows "Summoner" in menus and on the title screen, but the job plays nothing like retail SMN. Boom is specific to this server's relaunch.

## How it works

Boom plays like a melee DD that weaves elemental magic between weapon swings. Every spell you cast has a **chance to detonate** — the explosion fires as a separate hit on the target and nearby enemies. The bigger the spell, the bigger the chance and the bigger the blast.

There are no commands to trigger detonations. **Cast spells. They boom.**

## Spells and detonation

Boom has access to two tiers of nukes:

| Tier | Spells | Detonation chance | Blast multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier III** | Stone III, Water III, Aero III, Fire III, Blizzard III, Thunder III | 15% | 1× |
| **Ancient Magic** | Flare, Freeze, Tornado, Quake, Burst, Flood | 35% | 5× (+AoE blast radius) |

Ancient Magic detonations also hit nearby targets in a splash radius — use them in packs.

## Ignite

Casting any **Enspell** (Enfire, Enblizzard, Enaero, Enstone, Enthunder, Enwater) triggers **Ignite** for 60 seconds. While Ignite is active:
- Your nuke detonation chance is **floored at 45%** (up from 15%)
- The enspell's melee enchant keeps your weapons lit between casts

Ignite is the core rotation loop: **enspell → nuke → nuke → re-enspell.**

## Detonation damage

Detonation damage scales with your INT and Magic Attack, plus a base value, multiplied by the spell's blast multiplier. Ancient Magic blasts hit hard — they're designed to be your big moments, not spam. All damage matches the spell's element, so resistances apply.

## Job traits

While Boom (SMN) is your **main job**, you passively receive:

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| HP bonus | +800 |
| STR | +40 |
| DEX / VIT | +20 each |
| Attack% | +25% |
| Accuracy | +50 |
| Double Attack | +12% |
| Critical Hit Rate | +8% |
| Store TP | +40 |
| Staff skill | +250 (combat + WS access) |
| Elemental skill | +250 (nuke accuracy + damage) |
| Magic Attack | +40 |
| Magic Accuracy | +40 |
| Fast Cast | +25% |

Staff and Elemental skill are applied as direct additive mods, so they stack cleanly with gear. Fast Cast lets you weave nukes without killing your melee uptime.
