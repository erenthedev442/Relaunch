# Boom (Custom Job)

**Boom** is a custom job on this server — a staff-swinging melee/hybrid DD whose elemental spells randomly **detonate** for massive bonus damage. No pets. No avatars. Just you, a staff, and a very satisfying explosion.

!!! tip "Summary"
    A staff-wielding melee/hybrid DD on the Summoner slot. Cast elemental spells — they have a chance to **detonate** for big bonus damage. Casting an enspell opens an **Ignite** window that makes detonations far more reliable.

!!! warning "Relaunch exclusive"
    Boom occupies the Summoner job slot. The client still shows "Summoner" in menus and on the title screen, but the job plays nothing like retail SMN. Boom is specific to this server's relaunch.

## How it works

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="boom-overview" -->
Boom plays like a melee damage-dealer that weaves elemental magic between weapon swings. **Every spell you cast has a chance to detonate** — the explosion lands as a separate elemental hit on the target (and, for the biggest spells, the enemies around it).

The bigger the spell, the bigger the boom: your standard nukes detonate around **15%** of the time, while the heavy Ancient Magic detonates far more often (**35%**) and hits for about **5× as hard** with a splash radius.

There are no commands to trigger any of this. **Cast spells. They boom.** Casting an enspell opens an **Ignite** window that pushes every detonation chance up while it lasts.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="boom-overview" -->

Boom has access to two tiers of nukes — standard Tier-III elemental magic for steady damage, and the heavy **Ancient Magic** (Flare, Freeze, Tornado, Quake, Burst, Flood) for your big moments. Each spell carries its own detonation odds and blast size:

## Ignite

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="boom-ignite" -->
Casting any enspell (**Enfire**, **Enblizzard**, **Enaero**, **Enstone**, **Enthunder**, **Enwater**) triggers **Ignite** for **60 seconds**.

While Ignite is active:

- Every nuke's detonation chance is **floored at 45%** — your standard spells boom far more reliably.
- The enspell's own melee enchant keeps your weapon lit between casts, supporting the hybrid melee rhythm.

The core loop is **enspell → nuke → nuke → re-enspell** to keep the window open.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="boom-ignite" -->

## Detonation damage

Detonation damage scales with your **INT** and **Magic Attack**, plus a flat base value, multiplied by the spell's blast multiplier. It matches the spell's element, so resistances apply. Ancient Magic blasts hit hard — they're designed to be your big moments, not spam.
