# Weapon Forge

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="weapon-forge-intro" -->
The **Weapon Forger** in **Leafallia** upgrades weapons through three stages — 119I, 119II, and 119III — in the same style as retail FFXI's Oboro reforging. Each step consumes your current weapon and the required materials; nothing is lost until you confirm.

There are **14 weapon-type chains**, one per weapon skill. Start with a **119I weapon** from the Bronze vendor in Escha - Zi'Tah and work your way up to a **Stage-5 Relic** (119III).

!!! tip "How to find it"
    The Weapon Forger stands next to the Relic Forge in **{{npc:weapon_forger}}** (`!leaf`). It auto-detects which upgradeable weapons you have — if you own a 119I or 119II weapon the menu entry appears automatically.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="weapon-forge-intro" -->

## Costs

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="weapon-forge-costs" -->
| Step | Consume | Materials | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 119I → 119II | Your 119I weapon | 25× Kindreds Medal | Hunting League Rank III (Elite) |
| 119II → 119III | Your 119II weapon | 50× Demons Medal + 2,000 Reforge Marks (any pool) | Hunting League Rank V (Legend) |

**Medals** come from the Hunt Seals NPC in Escha - Zi'Tah (same vendor as Hunt Marks). **Reforge Marks** are earned from Reforge NM kills in Gwora Corridor — your total across all three pools (AF, Relic, Empyrean) counts.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="weapon-forge-costs" -->

## Tips

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="weapon-forge-tips" -->
**Dual path to 119III** — the Stage-5 Relics at the end of each chain are the same items sold by the **Relic Forge** NPC (Dynamis path). Both paths award the identical weapon; use whichever suits your play style.

**Slot the 119II first** — the 119II weapons (e.g., Raetic Algol, Crepuscular Knife) are solid mid-endgame pieces on their own. You don't have to push to 119III immediately.

**Silver vendor shortcut** — the 119II stage weapon is also sold directly at the Silver weapon vendor in Escha - Zi'Tah for 25× Kindred's Medals, so you can skip the forge step and still have a 119II to trade in later.

**Nothing is lost on cancel** — the NPC only takes items on the final confirm. Browsing the menu and reading the chain is always free.
