# Login Rewards

the Relaunch server rewards players who log in consistently. Two systems run side by side: a small daily bonus for any login, and escalating milestone bonuses for consecutive streaks.

---

## Daily Login Bonus

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="login-daily-bonus" -->
Every day you log in, you receive **+50 Hunt Marks** automatically. The bonus fires once per UTC day.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="login-daily-bonus" -->

Re-logging or zoning does not trigger it again.

A system message confirms the bonus a few seconds after you connect:

> *[Daily Bonus] +50 Hunt Marks for logging in today! Come back tomorrow for more.*

There is no action required. Just log in.

---

## Login Streak Bonus

On top of the daily bonus, the server tracks how many consecutive days you have logged in. Reaching a streak milestone awards a **one-time bonus** — and the server nudges you with how far you are from the next tier.

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="login-streak-milestones" -->
| Streak | One-Time Bonus |
|---|---|
| 7 days in a row | +75 Hunt Marks |
| 14 days in a row | +200 Hunt Marks |
| 21 days in a row | +400 Hunt Marks |
| 30 days in a row | +750 Hunt Marks |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="login-streak-milestones" -->

Each milestone reward is granted **once per threshold**. Reaching 30 days also triggers the 7-day and 14-day rewards (if you have not already claimed them).

### Streak Rules

- The streak increments when you log in on a new UTC calendar day.
- **Missing a day resets your streak to 1.** There is no grace period.
- Re-logging on the same day does not affect the streak count.
- Streak progress is displayed in your linkshell chat tab when you log in mid-streak.

### Example Nudge Messages

> *[Login Streak] 3 days in a row — 7 days earns +75 marks!*
>
> *[Login Streak] 7-Day Streak — 7 days in a row! +75 bonus marks!*

---

## Combined Value

The daily bonus and the streak milestones stack: every login pays the daily amount (see [Daily Login Bonus](#daily-login-bonus)), and crossing a streak threshold adds its one-time bonus on top (see [Login Streak Bonus](#login-streak-bonus)).

Streaks are a low-effort way to accumulate marks between active play sessions. Even a 10-minute login each day adds up.
