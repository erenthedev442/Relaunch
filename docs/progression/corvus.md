# Corvus — the Black Arrow Trust

**Corvus** is a custom Trust: a silent archer who takes up the back line and pours ranged damage into whatever you point him at. Buy him once and he's a permanent addition to your Trust roster — a pure damage dealer who asks for nothing and says even less.

!!! tip "Summary"
    A one-time, permanent ranged-damage Trust sold by the Void Keeper in GM Home — bind Corvus once, then summon him from your Trust menu as "Curilla".

## At a glance

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="corvus-summary" -->
- **Where:** the **Void Keeper** at [GM Home](gm-home.md) (reach it with `!gmhome`) — the same NPC that sells [Meat](meat.md) and [Gemma](skoll.md)
- **Cost:** **75,000,000 gil** — one-time, permanent, per character
- **Role:** ranged damage dealer — he stands at the back line and shoots
- **To summon:** cast **Curilla** from your Trust menu — that slot *is* Corvus (the name **Corvus** appears over his head and in your party list)
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="corvus-summary" -->

## What he does

Corvus is built around one job: ranged damage.

- **He shoots, you skillchain.** Corvus parks at the back line and rains ranged attacks and weapon skills on your target — a steady second source of damage that never needs to close to melee range.
- **He stays out of the way.** As a back-line damage dealer he keeps his distance and lets you and your tank hold the front.
- **Cheaper than the support trusts.** Where Meat and Gemma are premium pillars, Corvus is a more affordable pick-up — a damage boost you can bind early and keep forever.

## How to unlock

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="corvus-unlock" -->
Travel to **GM Home** with `!gmhome`, find the **Void Keeper**, and bind Corvus for **75,000,000 gil**. The binding is permanent and per character — buy him once and he's yours forever.

In your Trust menu he shows up as **"Curilla"** — that Trust slot was re-used, so the menu label is just cosmetic. Cast **Curilla**, and the archer who answers — named **Corvus** over his head — *is* him.

So: **cast Curilla → get Corvus.**
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="corvus-unlock" -->
