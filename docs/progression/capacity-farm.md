# Capacity Point Farm

Job Points are a long grind, and Vana'diel's wild capacity mobs are scattered
and slow to respawn. The **Capacity Point Farm** fixes that: a permanent,
always-up camp of high-level phantoms in Bibiki Bay that die fast and pop back
instantly, so your capacity chain never goes cold.

!!! tip "Summary"
    Type `!capacity` to warp to an always-up Capacity Point camp in Bibiki Bay —
    a pool of 100 Capacity Phantom (Lv150-160) that instant-respawn on
    death. Shared claim, no loot or gil, just Capacity Points.

## Getting there

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="capacity-farm-access" -->
Type **`!capacity`** from anywhere to warp straight to the Capacity Point farm in **Bibiki Bay**. It is open to every player, no flags or quests — the camp is always live, so you can drop in, grind a chain, and warp out whenever you like.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="capacity-farm-access" -->

## The camp

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="capacity-farm-mobs" -->
The camp keeps a pool of **100** always-up **Capacity Phantom** monsters standing by. They sit between **level 150 and 160**, and each one **instantly respawns the moment it dies** — kill one and a fresh phantom takes its place on the spot, so there is always a target and your capacity chain never lapses waiting on a pop.

The phantoms wear a rotating mix of **5** different monster looks, so the camp isn't a wall of identical models.

Claim is shared free-for-all — everyone at the camp can fight every monster, and the killer's alliance earns the Capacity Points. Bring a party, bring trusts, or solo it; the pool is sized to stay dense without ever running dry.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="capacity-farm-mobs" -->

## What you earn

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="capacity-farm-rewards" -->
Every phantom is a full Capacity Point kill. Because they die fast and respawn instantly, you can keep the engine's capacity chain hot the whole time — back-to-back kills inside the chain window stack the usual chain bonus on top of each award.

On top of the normal level-based award, each kill pays a **flat 2,000 bonus Capacity Points** to the killer (scaled by the server's Capacity rate, same as every other source).
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="capacity-farm-rewards" -->

## Good to know

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="capacity-farm-notes" -->
- **No loot, no gil.** The phantoms drop nothing — this is a pure Capacity Point camp, not a gear or gil farm.
- **Capacity Points only.** The level range is well above the Lv100 floor that makes a mob CP-eligible, so every kill counts toward your Job Points once you've earned Job Points access.
- **Always on.** The camp is seeded when the zone wakes and tops itself back up on every kill and every zone-in, so it is ready around the clock with no GM intervention.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="capacity-farm-notes" -->

Capacity Points feed your Job Points, so park here whenever you want to push a
job's gifts and Job Point categories — the chain stays hot as long as you keep
swinging.
