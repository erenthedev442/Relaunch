# Gear Finder

Search, filter and compare **every equippable item on the server**, or let the
**Set Builder** pick a best-in-slot loadout for your job. All data is pulled
straight from the live server, so what you see here is exactly what the game
uses.

!!! tip "How to use it"
    - **Database** — filter by slot, job, weapon type and level, then click
      **+ Add stat column** to bring any stat (Accuracy, Haste, Magic Attack,
      Refresh, …) in as a *sortable* column. Add a number to it to require
      `≥ that value`. Click any column header to sort; click a row to see its
      full stat list and where to get it.
    - **Set Builder** — choose a job and a priority (a role like *DD/Tank/
      Healer/Caster*, or a single stat), and get the top pick for every slot
      plus runners-up.
    - Tick **Obtainable here** to limit results to gear you can actually earn
      on the Relaunch server (vendors, augments, reforge, boards…).
    - Hover (or tap) any item name for its BG-Wiki preview.
