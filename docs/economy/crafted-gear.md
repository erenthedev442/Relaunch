# Crafted Gear

Some of the strongest equipment on the server never drops from a monster and
never sits on a vendor — it comes off a crafter's bench. This page is the
index of the **item level 100+** gear that synthesis produces, including the
craft-exclusive lines (Raetic, Arasy, Was, Oshosi, the job staves and more)
whose *only* source is crafting.

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="crafted-intro" -->
Crafting on the relaunch is a real gear pipeline: **4,603 synthesis recipes** are live, including the retail *escutcheon-master* lines that stock data ships disabled — here they are open to anyone at **skill 110** (the escutcheon key items are waived). The table below lists every **item level 100+** piece of equipment a crafter can make — **447 items**, many of them craft-exclusive (Raetic, Arasy, Was, Oshosi, the job staves and more exist nowhere else on the server). HQ tiers come from the same synth at higher quality; sub-99 crafted gear is also stocked cheaply by the Auction House market-maker.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="crafted-intro" -->

!!! tip "How to craft"
    Skill up the relevant craft the classic way — guild NPCs sell the low-tier
    ingredients, the Auction House market-maker stocks crystals and common
    materials, and recipes are the standard retail ones. The endgame synths
    below top out at **skill 110**.
