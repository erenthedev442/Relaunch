# Reforge Mark Exchange

The **Reforge Exchange** lets you convert spare Reforge marks from one line into another, so a surplus you've banked in one track can fund the set you're actually upgrading.

!!! tip "Summary"
    Trade excess AF, Relic, or Empyrean marks for the line you need at the Reforge Exchange near the Gear Vendors — a small conversion tax keeps each track honest.

## Where to find it

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="reforge-mark-exchange-access" -->
The **Reforge Exchange** stands at **Diorama Abdhaljs-Ghelsba**, right beside the Gear Vendors — the same spot where you turn marks into upgrades.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="reforge-mark-exchange-access" -->

## What it does

The [Reforge System](../progression/reforge.md) runs three parallel upgrade tracks — **AF**, **Relic**, and **Empyrean** — and each track earns and spends its own marks. Hunt long enough and you'll bank far more marks in one track than you can use while another sits short. This NPC lets you rebalance that surplus toward the set you're chasing.

## Conversions

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="reforge-mark-exchange-rates" -->
Each conversion charges a small tax, so trading down a surplus costs you a little but never trivializes a track:

| You spend | You receive | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 2 AF Marks | 1 Relic Marks | 2:1 |
| 2 Relic Marks | 1 Empy Marks | 2:1 |
| 3 AF Marks | 1 Empy Marks | 3:1 |
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="reforge-mark-exchange-rates" -->

## How to trade

Talk to the NPC and the menu lists each conversion with how many marks you currently hold. Pick a conversion, choose a batch size, and confirm — your source marks are spent and the new marks are added on the spot.

<!-- DOCGEN:BEGIN id="reforge-mark-exchange-batches" -->
Each visit you can convert in batches of **1, 5, 10, 25, 50** target marks at once (you'll only be offered batches you can afford), so a large surplus can be moved over in a few clicks.
<!-- DOCGEN:END id="reforge-mark-exchange-batches" -->
