# About the Relaunch

This is a **fresh-start server** — a clean wipe and rebuild of the FJB FFXI private server, redesigned from the ground up for long-term play.

---

## Why a Relaunch?

The original server proved the concept: FFXI with fast progression, a custom endgame, and a friendly community works. Players stayed for months. But over time, the economy inflated, veteran characters pulled far ahead of newcomers, and the gap between a day-one player and a year-one player became difficult to close.

The Relaunch fixes that. Everyone starts equal. The economy is fresh. The stat balance has been redesigned from scratch. And the content lineup has been expanded with systems that weren't in the original — Voidwatch, the Adventuring Fellow, Boom Job, Spell & Skill Mastery, and more.

---

## What's Different from the Original

**Redesigned stats and mob balance.** Enemy stats have been rebuilt from the ground up so that gear choices, skill investment, and build decisions all actually matter. The power curve is tighter and more deliberate.

**New relaunch-exclusive systems.** The Relaunch ships content that was never on the original server:

- **Voidwatch** — Planar Rifts scattered across 30 overworld zones, tier-scaling Voidwalker NM fights, and a Spectral Alignment reward system
- **Adventuring Fellow** — a personal companion any job can summon, leveled and built by the player
- **Boom Job** — a custom job on the Summoner slot: a staff-swinging hybrid DD whose spells randomly detonate
- **Spell & Skill Mastery** — permanent WS and spell power-ups earned from a rotating NM currency system
- **Affinity NM timed spawns** — all 24 Augment Sage affinity NMs placed as real timed spawns in their zones

**A living economy from day one.** The auction house market-maker seeds gear listings immediately at launch — no waiting months for the economy to bootstrap.

## What Carried Over

Everything that made the original server worth playing:

- **Fast progression, real endgame.** <!--setting:map.EXP_RATE-->3<!--/setting-->× mob EXP (and <!--setting:main.EXP_RATE:int-->10<!--/setting-->× from books, FoV/GoV & Records of Eminence) means you hit 99 in days, not months. But 99 is the *beginning*, not the finish line.

- **Hunting League — a custom 5-tier progression system.** Five ranks, each harder than the last, each with meaningful rewards and increasing HL Point rates.

- **Ascension — a prestige ladder above the level cap.** Reach Legend tier and re-clear the Nightmare Court — three ascension-only superbosses — to earn Ascension Points: permanent, stacking stat boosts tracked per main job.

- **Reforge system — three NM ladders to +3 BiS.** Kill Sky Gods for AF Marks. Kill Unity NMs for Relic Marks. Kill Abyssea NMs for Empy Marks. No RNG boxes, no cash shop — just kills.

- **Hunter's Guild — reputation that pays you back.** Four hunting guilds that amplify the marks every kill pays, the more of the world you hunt.

- **Augment Moogle — 400+ ways to customize your gear.** Catalysts from NM kills, permanent stat bonuses on your equipment.

- **Cross-Job Abilities — borrow from other jobs.** A curated set of job abilities usable on any job via macro.

- **No dead content — weekly objectives, daily board, Wave Master arena.** Always a reason to log in.

- **Endgame & Events.** Star-Devourer raid, Voidspire gauntlet, Colosseum, Invasions, Treasure Hunts, Chocobo Derby, Provisioners' League, Casino, seasonal events.

- **Prime Armory — claim an apex Prime weapon.** Trade a Prime Voucher for one of the server's top weapons.

- **Achievements & World Firsts.** Personal milestones and server-wide announcements for first-ever kills.

- **Leaderboards.** Live rankings across kills, augments, clears, and more.

- **A deep quality-of-life bench.** Always-popped NMs, Character Upgrader, homepoint healing, zone-in cutscene skips, sub-job EXP share, login streaks, and dozens more.

- **Everything unlocked at character creation.** Advanced jobs, outpost warps, maps — no friction.
